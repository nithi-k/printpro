Our Story

70 Years of Ink.
Now We Make the Merch.

Chaiyaboon Brothers Group has been the backbone of Thailand's screen printing industry since the 1950s — supplying inks to garment manufacturers, print shops, and textile businesses across Southeast Asia. PrintPro is CBG's dedicated merchandise production division, built to bring that same ink expertise directly into the world of event, artist, and brand merchandise.

How We Print

Three Technologies.
Endless Possibilities.

Direct-to-Garment

DTG Printing

Kornit DTG — mass production. Industrial-grade, photographic quality, high-volume runs with no color limits, water-based eco inks, no screens required.
Brother GTX — live on-site, small orders. Deployed at your festival: fans pick a design and watch it print in ~2 minutes.
Silk Screen Printing

Screen Print

All ink types, in-house — CBG manufactures plastisol, water-based, UV, discharge, metallic, glow, and puff inks. 70 years of formulation.
Production network — our ink clients are printing shops across Thailand. When demand scales, we activate this network for extra capacity on demand.
Sublimation Printing

Sublimation

DGI Printers — in-house equipment for high-resolution dye-sublimation: all-over prints, polyester garments, soft accessories, full-bleed designs.
Expanded capacity — additional sublimation capacity via our production partner network for large-scale or time-sensitive orders.

Sustainability

Made for a
Greener Merch Run.

01

Certified Ink Manufacturing

CBG's ink factory holds REACH, RoHS, and EN71 environmental certifications — meeting EU safety and chemical compliance standards across all ink formulations.

02

Low-VOC Ink Formulations

Our screen printing inks are formulated low-VOC — safer for production workers, printers, and end consumers, with reduced environmental impact during curing.

03

ECO TEX Certified DTG

All DTG printing on our Kornit machines uses ECO TEX certified inks exclusively — water-based, skin-safe, and certified for direct contact with garments.

04

Recycled & Organic Fabrics

rPET recycled polyester and organic cotton options available across all apparel SKUs — sourced through ArriveGuide and our SE Asia fabric mill network.
